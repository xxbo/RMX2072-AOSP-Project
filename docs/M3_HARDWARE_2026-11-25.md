Pinned ACK uses 320 + 16*n mV for ordinary PM8009 S2: neither 950 nor 952 mV

driver instead sends `ceil(min_uV/1000)` directly to RPMh, so its 950..952 mV
consumer vote cannot be copied as an ACK selector constraint.

Upstream [PM8009-1 correction](https://kernel.googlesource.com/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/stable/stable-queue/+/refs/tags/v6.12.41/releases/5.10.20/regulator-qcom-rpmh-regulator-add-pm8009-1-chip-revi.patch)
identifies the special SM8250 revision and its 900 + 16*n mV table. Trial 92
uses that compatible and the upstream RB5 900..952 mV constraint, retaining
board 20607's `smpf2` connection. This is a source-backed board inference, not
a fresh PMIC revision-register measurement. The driver's initial vote is
900 mV; this range stays within the OEM provider's 512..1100 mV limits and
does not raise the previous consumer ceiling. Successful registration and
endpoint discovery still require the next physical result. Offline validation
now also checks that every WLAN rail range contains an ACK voltage selector.

The next firmware inputs are [inventoried separately](../build/device/m3-wifi-firmware-source-2026-11-25.json):
