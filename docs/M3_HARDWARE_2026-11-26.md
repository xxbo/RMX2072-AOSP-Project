## Thermal and scheduling integration gaps

The current project DT monitors TSENS0 sensors 5 (cluster 0), 6 (cluster 1)
and 15 (GPU top). These are valid SM8250 cluster/GPU locations; they are not
the hottest-of-all-cores measurement. Per-core and GPU-bottom coverage still
need integration. Existing CPU and GPU cooling maps retain the bring-up
80 C passive and 100 C critical trips. Do not treat these bring-up thresholds
as a qualified enclosure, battery or sustained-performance thermal policy.

The source Power HAL advertises no boosts, hint sessions or headroom support.
That does not disable the kernel's schedutil/devfreq governors, but it leaves
Android performance-hint integration open. There is no project Thermal HAL.
The [Android thermal interface](https://source.android.com/docs/core/power/thermal-mitigation)
also needs real sensor/severity reporting and callbacks; readable kernel
temperature files alone do not establish framework thermal integration.
